Why your laptop slows down

It's probably not broken

Most "slow" laptops just need a clean. Here's what's actually happening.

Dust clogs everything
Fans and vents fill up over time, trapping heat inside. Your laptop can't breathe and throttles to protect itself.
Thermal paste dries out
Usually after 3–5 years. When it fails, heat can't transfer properly from the CPU — causing throttling and slowdowns.
CPU throttling kills performance
When overheating, your CPU drops to a fraction of its power — sometimes as low as 10% — to avoid damage.
You don't need a new laptop
Most laptops people replace just need a good clean and repaste. A $72 service beats a $1,000 replacement.
